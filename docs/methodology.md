# Methodology

Only current indexable publication snapshots are included. Each service and billing unit is kept separate. A city guide range is a planning estimate, not a measured citywide average.

Affordability uses the midpoint of the same-scope low and high estimates. One purchase divided by median annual household income or median monthly gross rent gives the corresponding share. Missing, annotated or incompatible records are excluded. Rankings can be affected by Census survey uncertainty.

Cities are a publication sample, not a nationally representative survey. Census periods and margins of error are included in JSON.
